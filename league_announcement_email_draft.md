Here is a quick tour of what’s in it and how you can use it to get an edge:

---

### 1. ⚖️ "Start 'Em / Sit 'Em" Head-to-Head Comparison Tool
Torn between two defenses on Sunday morning?
* Tap the **`⚖️ Start 'Em / Sit 'Em`** button at the top of the page.
* Pick any two defenses side-by-side to compare projected points, safe floors, projected sacks, opponent turnovers, Vegas implied totals, and weather.
* The tool gives you an automated, data-driven start recommendation and highlights specific metric advantages.

---

### 2. 📋 1-Click "Copy Cheat Sheet" for Quick Sharing
* Hit the **`📋 Copy Cheat Sheet`** button in the header to instantly grab a clean, emoji-formatted Week 9 summary copied straight to your clipboard (smash plays, priority streamers, weather alerts, and top stashes).

---

### 3. 🛫 3-Week Multi-Week "Stream-Ahead" Runway (Weeks 9–11)
Tired of burning waiver wire claims every single Tuesday?
* On the main **Week 9 Projections table**, check the new **3-Week Runway** column.
* Every defense gets a difficulty grade (`A+` down to `D`) and color-coded schedule badges for Weeks 9, 10, and 11 (Green = Easy, Yellow = Neutral, Red = Tough, Grey = BYE).
* Sort by this column to find a defense you can comfortably start for multiple weeks in a row.

---

### 4. 🏆 Fantasy Playoff Schedule Radar (Weeks 15–17)
Why wait until December to think about the postseason?
* Head to the **Week 10 Look-Ahead Stashes** tab to find the **Fantasy Playoff Schedule Radar**.
* Ranks all 32 NFL defenses by their schedule difficulty during the fantasy playoffs: Quarterfinals (Week 15), Semifinals (Week 16), and Championship (Week 17).
* Defenses are grouped into **Elite (Tier 1)**, **Favorable (Tier 2)**, **Moderate (Tier 3)**, and **Tough (Tier 4)** tiers with strategic stashing advice so you can lock down championship-winning matchups early.

---

### 5. 🎯 Master Offense Vulnerability & Defense Momentum Boards
* **Offense Vulnerability (The "Target" Board):** Heatmap tracking every fantasy point yielded by all 32 NFL offenses across Weeks 1–8. *(For example: Cleveland has surrendered an NFL-high **41 fantasy points** to opposing defenses over their last 4 games alone—over 10 PPG!)*
* **Defense Momentum Board:** Tracks how every NFL defense is actually scoring, featuring an Excel-style blue (boom) to red (quiet) heatmap, sack counts, and a **Surging / Steady / Cooling** momentum indicator showing whether a defense is trending up or slowing down.

---

### 6. 🔬 D/ST Fantasy Points Anatomy & Weekly Projections
* **Points Anatomy:** Breaks down all **1,181.0 defensive fantasy points** scored in the NFL this season. Sacks drive **47.2%** of all scoring in our format, followed by low-scoring game brackets (15.2%), INTs (14.4%), and fumble recoveries (11.4%).
* **Weekly Scouting Reports:** Click on **"Full Analysis ➔"** or tap any row to open a pop-up matchup card with a complete strategic breakdown, pressure rates, QB sack rates when pressured, and kickoff weather alerts (like 20+ mph wind gusts that boost INT chances).
* **Model Accuracy Report Card:** We even included a transparent audit tab tracking the model's accuracy, hit rates, and scoring floor reliability against actual NFL box scores.
