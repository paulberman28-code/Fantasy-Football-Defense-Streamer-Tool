Here is a quick rundown of what’s in it and how you can use it:

---

### 1. Master Offense Vulnerability History (The "Target" Board)
Ever wonder which offenses are bleeding points to opposing defenses? 
* **The Heatmap:** This tab tracks every fantasy point allowed by all 32 NFL offenses across Weeks 1–8.
* **The Cleveland Browns Example:** Look at Cleveland on this board—they’ve surrendered an NFL-high **41 fantasy points** to opposing defenses over their last 4 games alone (over 10 PPG!), making whoever plays them an automatic high-upside target. You can spot similar emerging trends and cold streaks across the league at a glance.

---

### 2. D/ST Fantasy Points Anatomy: What Drives the Best Defenses?
What actually separates an elite fantasy defense from an average one under our league's scoring system? 
* We broke down all **1,181.0 defensive fantasy points** scored across the NFL this season.
* **The Sacks Factor:** Sacks account for **47.2%** of all defensive fantasy scoring in our format, followed by low-scoring game brackets (15.2%), INTs (14.4%), and fumble recoveries (11.4%).
* You can see the exact scoring DNA of every NFL defense to see whether a team relies on volatile return touchdowns or a consistent, high-floor pass rush.

---

### 3. Weekly Projections & In-Depth Matchup Metrics (Week 9)
Instead of relying on gut feelings, this tab tiers defenses into **Priority**, **Speculative**, **Neutral**, and **Avoid**:
* **Baseline + Advanced Metrics:** Projected fantasy points, floor ratings, Vegas implied team totals, and defensive pressure rates paired with Next Gen Stats (Quarterback sack rate when pressured, opponent pass EPA, 3rd-down stops, and time-to-throw).
* **Weather & Injury Flags:** Highlights game-time weather factors (like 20+ mph wind gusts that spike interception probabilities) and real-time defensive injuries.
* **Full Scouting Reports:** Click on **"Full Analysis ➔"** or tap any row to open a pop-up matchup card with a complete strategic breakdown for that game.

---

### 4. Week 10 Look-Ahead Stash Board (Beat the Waiver Wire)
Why spend FAAB or waste waiver priority on Wednesday morning when you can stash next week’s smash play right now for free? 
* This tab projects the upcoming week's top defensive streaming targets so you can stash them on your bench a week early before the rest of the league catches on.
